Payout amounts are editable — click into any number to override it. % of Pool and Jump update live, and the line above flags if your edits no longer add up to the prize pool.

1st through 9th always get individual payouts (or all paid places, if fewer than 9 cash). Beyond 9th, places are grouped into tiers the way most rooms post them (e.g. "10th–12th"). Every jump — individual or tier-to-tier — is strictly larger than the jump below it, and the last paid place always pays exactly the min-cash multiplier × the buy-in (2× minimum, adjustable above) unless the pool can't support it, in which case you'll see a warning above. Raising the multiplier shifts money from the top places into the lower bands.
